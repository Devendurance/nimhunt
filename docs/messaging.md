# NimHunt Messaging System

**Working product name:** NimHunt  
**Host experience:** Nimiq Pay Mini App  
**Product type:** Mobile-first, skill-based daily treasure adventure  
**Primary world:** Angkor Ruins  
**Core reward:** Configurable real NIM reward, subject to daily reward slots and eligibility  
**Recommended campaign line:** **Explore. Survive. Seal.**

> **Important positioning rule:** NIM is earned by completing the expedition's visible skill task alive and sealing an available claim. Random chest contents affect exploration and progression; they do not determine real-NIM eligibility.

This document is the working verbal identity and messaging architecture for NimHunt. The name remains a working title and may change without changing the product strategy.

---

## 1. Executive Diagnosis

NimHunt should not be presented as a crypto app with a game attached.

It should be presented as:

> **A daily skill expedition where real NIM becomes the treasure at the end of a successful run.**

The game's strongest commercial and emotional idea is the combination of:

- a short, replayable exploration challenge;
- a clearly stated task before play begins;
- limited daily reward slots;
- a real Nimiq Pay wallet-signing moment;
- a memorable fiction layer involving the ruins, the Goblin, and the Temple Vault.

The player should remember:

> **I had a mission, I survived the ruins, and I sealed real treasure.**

They should not leave thinking:

> “It was a random crypto loot box.”

## 2. Brand Foundation

### Product idea in one sentence

**NimHunt is a mobile-first daily treasure adventure inside Nimiq Pay where players complete skill-based expedition tasks, survive compact ruin puzzles, and seal available NIM rewards with their Nimiq wallet.**

### Product fantasy

The player is not navigating a financial interface. They are entering a living ruin.

Every day:

- the hunt resets;
- a limited number of real NIM treasures are available;
- the player receives a visible expedition task;
- the player explores, collects, avoids hazards, and solves small environmental puzzles;
- a successful run unlocks the right to seal an available treasure;
- Nimiq Pay signing becomes the fictional act of sealing the vault.

### Purpose

Make a real wallet action feel understandable, exciting, and earned by placing it inside a short skill adventure.

### Vision

A world where digital money can be discovered through playful, fair, wallet-native experiences rather than presented as an abstract transaction.

### Mission

Give Nimiq Pay users a daily reason to return, explore, complete a deterministic challenge, and experience NIM as something tangible: treasure that can be sealed and moved into their balance.

### Category

The clearest public category is:

> **A skill-based daily treasure hunt with real NIM rewards.**

Use `Nimiq Pay Mini App` as the distribution and wallet context, not as the category itself.

### Emotional payoff

> “I earned this. I found the route, survived the danger, and sealed the treasure myself.”

### Strategic enemy

**Passive reward mechanics that disguise chance as achievement.**

NimHunt should stand against:

- empty token farming;
- random prize boxes presented as skill;
- confusing wallet flows;
- financial jargon replacing play;
- rewards with no memorable moment attached.

### Brand personality

- Adventurous
- Fair
- Playful
- Slightly cheeky
- Tactile
- Competitive without becoming hostile
- Clear about rules and rewards

## 3. Audience and Jobs-to-be-Done

### Primary audience: Nimiq Pay users

People already inside the Nimiq ecosystem who need a simple, entertaining reason to open the Mini App and interact with their wallet.

**Job:**

> “Give me something fun and clear to do with my Nimiq wallet today, then make the reward moment feel real.”

### Secondary audience: mobile puzzle and adventure players

Players who enjoy short grid-based exploration, environmental puzzles, hazards, collectibles, and repeatable daily challenges.

**Job:**

> “Give me a compact skill challenge I can understand quickly, improve at, and replay tomorrow.”

### Tertiary audience: crypto-curious users

People who may find wallets and blockchain intimidating but understand treasure, missions, signatures, scarcity, and a visible balance update.

**Job:**

> “Let me experience the useful part of a wallet action without making me learn crypto infrastructure first.”

### Competitive and social audience

Players who care about:

- points;
- streaks;
- daily completion;
- leaderboards;
- Monthly Hero recognition;
- public achievement cards.

**Job:**

> “Give me a daily challenge worth mastering and a visible way to prove I did well.”

### What players need to believe

- The task is visible before movement begins.
- Success depends on skill and clearly stated rules.
- Random chest contents do not decide whether real NIM is earned.
- There are only a limited number of daily reward slots.
- The wallet-signing moment is understandable and purposeful.
- A failed run does not secretly spend money or create a misleading claim.
- If rewards are exhausted, the game says so clearly and still gives the player meaningful progression.

## 4. Positioning

### Positioning statement

> For Nimiq Pay users and mobile adventure players who want a short, meaningful daily challenge, NimHunt is a skill-based treasure adventure that turns successful exploration into a real NIM sealing moment. Unlike random reward boxes, idle claim systems, or crypto dashboards, NimHunt makes the reward depend on a visible task, survival, and a wallet action the player can understand.

### Simple positioning version

> **NimHunt is a daily skill hunt where you explore, survive, and seal real NIM treasure.**

### Value proposition

Complete a clearly stated expedition task, survive the ruins, and—while daily reward slots remain—seal a real NIM reward through Nimiq Pay.

### Differentiation

NimHunt combines four things that are rarely presented as one experience:

1. A compact, skill-based exploration game.
2. A daily scarcity model with a visible reward count.
3. A real wallet-signing action integrated into the fiction.
4. A reward and leaderboard system that separates skill from random loot.

### Reasons to believe

Use only where implemented and verifiable:

- Every reward-eligible run begins with a visible Expedition Task.
- Task completion is evaluated using deterministic conditions.
- The map must guarantee reachable objectives for tasks such as Gem Runner.
- Random chest contents do not directly determine real-NIM eligibility.
- Nimiq Pay handles the wallet-signing step.
- The backend verifies task completion, eligibility, and signature before payout.
- Reward slots, expedition count, and reset state are visible to the player.
- A completed claim produces a visible reward and balance-confirmation state.

## 5. Messaging House

### Roof: core message

> **A daily skill adventure where real NIM is earned by completing the mission—not by luck.**

This is the message that should survive the landing page, game UI, social post, demo narration, and prize explanation.

### Pillar 1 — Every expedition has a mission

**Message:** Know what success means before you take the first step.

**Proof points:**

- The Expedition Task appears before movement begins.
- Tasks use clear archetypes such as Gem Runner, Vault Breaker, Chest Hunter, Relic Keeper, and Survivor.
- The Daily Expedition Board can present the same task structure to all players.
- The player can understand what must be completed before entering danger.

**Player value:** The run feels fair, purposeful, and replayable.

### Pillar 2 — Skill earns the real treasure

**Message:** Explore well, survive the hazards, complete the task, and earn the right to seal.

**Proof points:**

- Real-NIM eligibility comes from deterministic task completion while alive.
- Reachable map objectives are guaranteed where required.
- Chests provide bonus exploration outcomes, not the cash-prize decision.
- A failed run is a game outcome, not a random financial loss.

**Player value:** The reward feels earned rather than arbitrary.

### Pillar 3 — The wallet action becomes part of the adventure

**Message:** Nimiq Pay signing is the moment you seal the treasure.

**Proof points:**

- The Temple Vault or task completion reveals the claim state.
- The player chooses `seal treasure`.
- Nimiq Pay requests the wallet signature.
- Successful verification completes the claim flow.
- The reward animation connects the fictional treasure to the player's displayed balance.

**Player value:** A technical wallet action becomes a memorable game moment.

### Pillar 4 — Every day creates a reason to return

**Message:** The hunt resets, the board changes, and the ruins keep a record of your progress.

**Proof points:**

- Three free reward-eligible expeditions per day.
- A visible daily reward-slot count.
- Reset countdown.
- Gems, points, streaks, and leaderboards.
- Monthly Hero recognition and Hall of Heroes.

**Player value:** The game creates a daily ritual without requiring paid attempts or hidden economic pressure.

## 7. The Skill-Based Reward Message

This section is mandatory messaging for the landing page, onboarding, task board, reward claim flow, and competition presentation.

### The central distinction

> **Chest loot is exploration and progression. NIM is earned by completing the expedition's stated skill task alive.**

### Preferred wording

- `69 daily reward slots for completed skill tasks`
- `Complete the mission. Survive the run. Seal an available reward.`
- `Your task is shown before you move.`
- `Random chest contents can change your run, but they do not decide NIM eligibility.`
- `If the reward slots are gone, the vault still holds relic progress—but no NIM claim.`

### Avoid

- `69 chances to win`
- `Spin for NIM`
- `Lucky treasure boxes`
- `Random NIM drops`
- `Win crypto from chests`
- `The luckiest explorer wins`
- `Guaranteed NIM`
- `Free money`

### Pre-run disclosure

Use a compact, visible statement before movement begins:

> **Today's task:** collect 12 gems and escape alive.  
> Complete the task to become reward-eligible while NIM treasures remain. Chest contents do not determine NIM eligibility.

### Reward availability disclosure

> **NIM treasures remaining: 43 / 69**  
> Reward slots are limited and claimed in completion order after a verified task and wallet seal.

Use the exact operational rule implemented by the reward service. Do not imply a reservation until a reservation actually exists.

### Reward exhausted disclosure

> **Today's NIM treasures are sealed.**  
> Keep exploring for gems, relic progress, points, and leaderboard position. No NIM claim is available from this run.

Do not show a misleading `claim` button when slots are exhausted.

Role: player avatar and emotional guide.

Voice:

- curious;
- brave;
- lightly cheeky;
- never overconfident;
- celebrates effort, not luck.

Example lines:

- `That gem was worth the detour.`
- `You made it out with 18 hp. Respect.`
- `The Vault is close. Keep moving.`

### The Goblin

Role: chaos mascot, claim thief, and error-state character.

Voice:

- greedy;
- mischievous;
- funny rather than frightening;
- never allowed to obscure the real reward state.

Example lines:

- `Too slow, explorer!`
- `That treasure almost had my name on it.`
- `I can steal the drama, not the rules.`
- `No seal, no treasure. Your move.`

Avoid letting the Goblin imply that a random event decided the reward. The player must understand exactly what happened.

### The Golem

Role: future guardian and world-expansion symbol.

Voice:

- sparse;
- ancient;
- deliberate;
- not needed for complex Cycle 2 dialogue.

### NIM Treasure Coin

The treasure should feel like an ancient object first and a crypto asset second.

Use language such as:

- `golden treasure`
- `sealed NIM`
- `the vault's prize`
- `a real NIM reward`

Avoid leading with:

- tokenomics;
- yield;
- investment;
- financial upside;
- coin speculation.

## 13. Claims Discipline and Fairness Language

### Use only where implemented

Use:

- `skill-based daily adventure`;
- `visible task before the run`;
- `limited daily reward slots`;
- `eligible completed task`;
- `Nimiq Pay wallet signature`;
- `reward service verifies the run`;
- `gems are non-cash progression`;
- `chest loot does not determine NIM eligibility`;
- `reward amount is configured by the competition build`.

### Avoid

- `guaranteed NIM`;
- `win crypto every day`;
- `risk-free rewards`;
- `jackpot`;
- `lottery`;
- `spin to win`;
- `luckiest player wins`;
- `easy money`;
- `passive income`;
- `instant payout` unless technically confirmed;
- `everyone gets paid`;
- `the more you spend, the better your odds`;
- `gems are worth [fixed NIM amount]`.

### Required clarity statements

Where the claim flow appears:

> Reward eligibility requires completion of the displayed skill task while reward slots remain, followed by the required wallet-signing and verification steps.

Where chest content appears:

> Chest contents are gameplay outcomes. They do not determine eligibility for a real NIM reward.

Where reward slots are exhausted:

> Today's NIM rewards are no longer available. You can still earn points, relic progress, streak progress, and leaderboard position.

### Competition-rule note

The skill-based framing in this document follows the supplied brief. Before public launch, confirm all reward, promotional, wallet, eligibility, jurisdictional, and competition-language requirements against the official competition rules and applicable legal review.

> “The mission was visible from the start. The reward came from completing the skill task, not from a random chest. Nimiq Pay made the final signature feel like sealing a real treasure.”

The competition build should prove these three moments exceptionally well:

1. **Task clarity:** the player knows exactly what success requires before moving.
2. **Skill completion:** the player completes the route and task while alive.
3. **Wallet seal:** the player signs the available claim and sees the correct confirmed reward state.

Do not bury these moments beneath extra worlds, complex combat, elaborate crafting, or a large economy.

## 15. Messaging Tests

### Five-second comprehension

Show the hero for five seconds and ask:

1. What is NimHunt?
2. What do you do?
3. What is the reward?
4. Is the reward random or earned?

Desired answer:

> It is a daily skill treasure hunt where you complete a task and can seal real NIM.

### Task comprehension

Show the pre-run board and ask:

- What must you do?
- What ends the run?
- What happens if you fail?
- Can chest loot decide the NIM reward?

The player should answer without opening a help modal.

### Wallet-moment comprehension

After showing the seal screen, ask:

- What are you signing?
- Why are you signing it?
- Has the reward already been confirmed?
- What happens if the signature is rejected?

### Fairness comprehension

Show the chest system and ask:

> “What determines whether you receive real NIM?”

Correct answer:

> Completing the visible skill task alive while a reward slot remains, then completing the required seal and verification.

### Scarcity comprehension

Show the live hunt strip and ask:

- How many reward slots remain?
- When does the count reset?
- What happens when the count reaches zero?

### Emotional test

After a completed run, ask:

> “What was the most memorable moment?”

The desired answers should include:

- reaching the vault;
- surviving the Goblin;
- sealing the treasure;
- seeing NIM arrive.

If the main answer is “the chest was random,” the product story needs correction.

## 16. Final Recommendation

Lead with:

> **Explore the ruins. Seal the treasure.**

Clarify immediately:

> **NimHunt is a skill-based daily adventure inside Nimiq Pay where completing the mission can make you eligible to seal a real NIM reward.**

Use the compact brand loop everywhere:

> **Explore → Survive → Seal**

Use the fairness rule everywhere rewards are discussed:

> **Chest loot is for exploration. NIM is earned by the task.**

### Da Vinci verdict

**Promising and commercially memorable, with one non-negotiable messaging risk: reward ambiguity.**

- **Strongest asset:** The “seal the treasure” moment turns wallet signing into a piece of fiction players can understand.
- **Biggest weakness:** Daily scarcity, mystery chests, and real NIM can be misread as chance-based prize mechanics if the task distinction is not visible.
- **Highest-leverage change:** Put the Expedition Task and the skill-based eligibility rule before every reward moment.
- **Recommended direction:** A fair daily skill hunt with a memorable ruin world, a mischievous Goblin, and a wallet-native treasure seal.
- **Confidence:** High on the product story; medium on final public reward language until official rules and legal review are confirmed.
- **Test next:** Five-second comprehension, task clarity, skill-versus-chance comprehension, reward-exhausted comprehension, and whether players understand what the wallet signature actually does.
